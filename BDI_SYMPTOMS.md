# BDI-II Symptoms Reference

The Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II) consists of 21 symptoms. Each symptom can contribute 0-3 points to the total BDI-II score (range: 0-63).

## Complete List of BDI-II Symptoms

1. **Sadness** - Feelings of sadness, unhappiness, or being "down"

2. **Pessimism** - Negative outlook on the future, hopelessness

3. **Past Failure** - Feelings of having failed or disappointed others

4. **Loss of Pleasure** - Inability to experience pleasure or enjoyment

5. **Guilty Feelings** - Feelings of guilt or self-blame

6. **Punishment Feelings** - Feeling that one deserves punishment

7. **Self-Dislike** - Disliking or hating oneself

8. **Self-Criticalness** - Being overly critical of oneself

9. **Suicidal Thoughts or Wishes** - Thoughts of death or suicide

10. **Crying** - Crying spells or increased tearfulness

11. **Agitation** - Restlessness, inability to sit still

12. **Loss of Interest** - Loss of interest in previously enjoyed activities

13. **Indecisiveness** - Difficulty making decisions

14. **Worthlessness** - Feelings of being worthless or having no value

15. **Loss of Energy** - Decreased energy, feeling drained

16. **Changes in Sleeping Pattern** - Insomnia or hypersomnia

17. **Irritability** - Increased irritability or anger

18. **Changes in Appetite** - Decreased or increased appetite

19. **Concentration Difficulty** - Trouble concentrating or focusing

20. **Tiredness or Fatigue** - Feeling tired or fatigued

21. **Loss of Interest in Sex** - Decreased interest in sexual activity

## BDI-II Severity Levels

- **0-13**: Minimal depression
- **14-19**: Mild depression
- **20-28**: Moderate depression
- **29-63**: Severe depression

## Usage in Task

For each persona, participants must:
1. Estimate the BDI-II score (0-63)
2. Identify up to 4 key symptoms from the list above

The symptoms should be selected based on what is most prominent or significant in the persona's conversation.

## Detection Strategy

The system detects symptoms through:
- Keyword pattern matching
- Semantic similarity analysis
- Context understanding
- Sentiment analysis

Symptoms are ranked by confidence score, and the top 4 (above threshold) are selected as key symptoms.
